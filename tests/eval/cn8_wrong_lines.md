- **Description**: the eval set's goods description, which the model received verbatim inside a data block. Line breaks are shown as `<br>`, trailing spaces are dropped and Markdown characters are escaped.
- **Label paths**: `label_path(hs6)` (heading › subheading) followed by the CN-8 line's own path from `cn8_lines(hs6)`, both from `rag/nomenclature.py`.
- **Stated reason**: `cn8_eval.json` does not store the model's rationale. The column shows the two fields it does store: the evidence the model quoted (first sentence) and, for ask_shipper, the question.
- **Retrieval is not logged per line.** `cn8_eval.json` records only the ruling the model cited, not the up to 8 rulings `select_rulings` sent. When at most 7 rulings of other CN-8 lines can be sent before the first ruling at the expected CN-8, that ruling was sent in every run (none of the six failed), whatever the embedding order. "no" would mean the pool holds no ruling at the expected CN-8. "retrieval not logged": with this pool the answer depends on the embedding ranking, which was not logged.
