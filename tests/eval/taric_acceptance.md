3. **B, measures**: back on **Suchkriterien**, untick the box, enter one 10-digit code from the row in **Codenummer** and `CN` in **Ursprungsland**, and click **Suche starten**. On **Maßnahmen und Hinweise**, the **Einfuhrmaßnahmen** table may span several pages:

   - where the duty reads "Weitere Informationen siehe Bedingungen", open **Bedingungen** (company-specific anti-dumping rates with document D008);

   - rows for other countries and groups, such as preferences 142 or 106 for TR, AD and SM, are outside the lookup.

In **Finding**, write `match`, `mismatch: <what differs>` or `gap: <the expected known-limitation row>`.
